RunnerPdf

Every PDF tool, truly free. No limits, no ads, no uploads.

RunnerPdf does all its work on your Android phone. The app has no internet access at all, so your documents cannot be sent anywhere.

What it does

  • Organise: merge, split, extract, delete and rotate pages, crop.
  • Convert: pictures to PDF, PDF to pictures, text to PDF, Excel to PDF, PDF to Word.
  • Scan documents to searchable PDF with on-device text recognition.
  • Edit: sign, annotate and highlight, fill forms, watermark, page numbers.
  • Protect and unlock with a password, redact, compress, compare two PDFs.

Languages

English, Italiano, Español, Deutsch, Français.
